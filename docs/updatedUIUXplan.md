1. INTRODUCTION
The Startup Jigawa Digital Innovation Center website will serve as the official digital presence of the organization and as a central platform for communicating its programmes, opportunities, innovation activities, impact, research, partnerships and community initiatives.
The design should position Startup Jigawa as a credible development, innovation and technology institution, rather than as a conventional technology startup.
The website must communicate:
•	Credibility
•	Innovation
•	Community impact
•	Professionalism
•	Accessibility
•	Institutional trust
•	Youth and entrepreneurial energy
•	Evidence-based development
•	Technology and digital transformation
The interface should feel modern and digital without becoming overly futuristic or visually complicated.
________________________________________
2. DESIGN OBJECTIVES
The website design should:
1.	Clearly communicate what Startup Jigawa does.
2.	Make programmes and opportunities easy to discover.
3.	Showcase measurable impact.
4.	Present innovation and technology projects professionally.
5.	Build trust with government institutions, development partners and private organizations.
6.	Remain accessible to young people and community members.
7.	Provide a strong content structure that can grow over time.
8.	Support CMS-driven content management.
9.	Work effectively across mobile, tablet and desktop devices.
10.	Establish a consistent and recognizable Startup Jigawa visual identity.
________________________________________
3. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
The central design principle is:
Credible enough for government and development partners. Accessible enough for young people and communities. Modern enough to represent technology and innovation.
The website should avoid looking like:
•	A generic SaaS landing page
•	A cryptocurrency website
•	A futuristic AI platform
•	A gaming website
•	A technology template
•	A portfolio website
•	An overly corporate banking website
Instead, the design should feel like a serious development and innovation institution with a strong digital identity.

6. COLOR USAGE PRINCIPLE
The website should not be predominantly green.
Recommended visual balance:
•	White: dominant
•	Off-white: secondary background
•	Black: typography and structural contrast
•	Green: accent and action color
Green should communicate action, growth and identity, rather than being used as a background everywhere.
Avoid:
•	Green gradients everywhere
•	Neon green
•	Excessive green cards
•	Glowing green effects
•	Green text on green backgrounds
•	Overly saturated visual effects

7. TYPOGRAPHY
7.1 Primary Typeface
Recommended primary font:
Plus Jakarta Sans
It provides a modern, professional and human appearance suitable for the desired institutional aesthetic.
Fallback
Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif
The implementation should ensure that the website remains visually consistent if the preferred font is unavailable.

41. VISUAL QUALITY STANDARD
Every page should pass the following test:
Does it feel institutional?
Does it feel human?
Does it feel modern?
Is the information easy to understand?
Is there enough whitespace?
Are the headings strong?
Are the images authentic?
Are the CTAs obvious?
Does it work on mobile?
Does it look like Startup Jigawa rather than a generic template?
If the answer to these questions is no, the design should be refined before implementation is considered complete.

43. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
The UI/UX implementation is considered successful when:
1.	The Startup Jigawa identity is immediately recognizable.
2.	The website feels like an established development and innovation institution.
3.	Typography has a strong editorial hierarchy.
4.	MUMSA's quality level is achieved without copying its design.
5.	Brand colors are used consistently.
6.	Mobile and desktop layouts are equally strong.
7.	Programmes and opportunities are easy to discover.
8.	Impact is clearly communicated.
9.	Content is structured for CMS management.
10.	Accessibility requirements are addressed.
11.	Components are reusable.
12.	The website does not look like a generic AI-generated website.
13.	The website avoids unnecessary visual effects.
14.	The interface remains fast and readable.
15.	Every major page follows the same design language.
