ADMIN CMS IS REQUIRED

Build a secure, responsive administrative CMS as part of the website architecture. The CMS must allow authorized administrators to manage the major content displayed on the public website without modifying source code.

The CMS must support management of Programmes, Opportunities, Events, News, Research & Publications, Innovation Labs, Products & Solutions, Sectors, Impact Stories, Partners, Team Members, Homepage Content, Impact Statistics, Media and Site Settings.

Implement authentication, role-based access control, draft/published states, validation, media management, search/filtering and appropriate audit information.

The public website must consume published CMS content through the approved backend/API architecture.

Do not expose administrative functionality to unauthenticated public users.

Do not hard-code CMS-managed content unnecessarily into frontend components.

The CMS interface should be functional, clean and professional rather than overly designed. Its priority is usability for Startup Jigawa administrators.
