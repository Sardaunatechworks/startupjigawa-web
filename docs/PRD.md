Document Purpose
This PRD defines the business goals, user needs, information architecture, functional scope, content 
model, administration requirements, quality expectations and acceptance criteria for the Startup 
Jigawa Digital Innovation Center Website. It is intended to guide product, design, content, engineering 
and management teams through a shared implementation scope.
Institutional positioning
The website must not present Startup Jigawa as only a training centre or only a software company. The 
organization profile positions Startup Jigawa as a digital innovation, civic technology and development 
institution connecting talent, technology, entrepreneurship, research, civic participation, institutions and 
communities.
Institutional source references: Company Profile sections 1, 5 and 9.
1. Product Context
Startup Jigawa Ltd is based in Dutse, Jigawa State and has operated since 2017. The company profile 
reports nine years of operation, 50,000+ people trained, 12+ institutional partners and five core sectors. 
The website must translate this institutional breadth into a clear digital experience that works for 
young people, entrepreneurs, government institutions, development partners, businesses, civil society 
and the wider public.
1.1 Product Vision
Create a credible, accessible and modern institutional platform that makes Startup Jigawa easy to 
understand, easy to engage with and easy to verify, while giving the organization a practical system for 
publishing programs, opportunities, innovation work, research, impact and partnership information.
1.2 Product Goals
• Establish a clear institutional identity and strengthen public trust.
• Make programs, opportunities and events discoverable and actionable.
• Show verified impact, products, research and partnerships without overstating unverified claims.
• Provide a sustainable CMS so staff can update content without developer intervention.
• Support partnership, training, media and general enquiries through structured contact flows.
• Create a low-bandwidth, mobile-first experience suitable for users across Jigawa State and beyond.
• Provide a foundation for future applicant, alumni, LMS, startup directory and open-data capabilities.

21. Constraints, Dependencies and Content Gaps
Do not invent missing institutional facts
The profile explicitly flags entity type/incorporation date, TIN/licences, leadership/board details, staff 
headcount, product deployment status, exact partnership status, formal policies, financial statements, facility 
capacity, awards/media references and testimonials for confirmation. The launch content process must verify 
these items before public publication.
Institutional source references: Company Profile sections 2, 3, 7, 8, 18, 23, 26, 28, 29, 34 and 37.
22. Product Acceptance Criteria
• A new visitor can understand Startup Jigawa’s institutional role from the homepage without opening 
the company profile.
• A user can locate and act on an open opportunity in three or fewer meaningful navigation steps.
• Staff can create and publish a program, event, article and publication without code changes.
• Unverified impact or partnership information cannot accidentally appear as verified public fact 
through the default workflow.
• All key public pages work cleanly on mobile, tablet and desktop.
• No critical accessibility, security or broken-link issues remain at launch.
• SEO metadata, sitemap, robots directives and social previews are present for public content.
• Forms provide confirmation, validation and internal routing.
• The site maintains usable performance on constrained mobile connections.
